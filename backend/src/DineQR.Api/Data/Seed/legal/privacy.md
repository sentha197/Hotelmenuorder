# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information {{restaurant.name}} ("the restaurant", "we", "us"), {{restaurant.address}}, collects when you use the DineQR ordering service, why, and what your choices are. The restaurant decides how this information is used and is responsible for it.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to take, prepare and bill your orders:

- **Ordering session:** a random session identifier, the table number (or takeaway), and when the session started and expires.
- **Orders:** the dishes, options and quantities you order, any special instructions or coupon code you enter, prices, taxes, discounts and totals, and the order's status history.
- **Bills and payments:** amounts, payment method, payment status and references (see section 9).
- **Feedback:** the rating and any comment you choose to leave about an order.
- **Network check:** your internet address, while the restaurant's Wi-Fi restriction is switched on (see section 6).
- **Location check:** your device's position when you order, only while the restaurant's location restriction is switched on, checked and not stored (see section 7).
- **Your acceptance of our documents:** which version of these documents you accepted and when (see section 3).

## 2. Customer Personal Information

You do not create an account, and the app does not ask for your name, phone number, email address, date of birth or any identity document. Please do not include personal details in special instructions or feedback comments; if you do, they are stored with the order.

## 3. Account and Authentication Information

There are no customer accounts or passwords. When you scan a QR code, the server gives your device a temporary session token for that table (or for takeaway). It expires after a few hours and only lets you order for that table and see your own orders and bills. When you accept the Terms & Conditions and acknowledge this Privacy Policy, we record the session, the table, the document and its version, and the date and time, as evidence of what you agreed to.

## 4. Order and Transaction Details

Your orders, bills, payments and refunds are kept with the session and table they belong to, so that the kitchen can prepare them, staff can serve and bill them, and the restaurant can keep the accounts and tax records the law requires.

## 5. Device and Network Information

- **On your device:** the app keeps your session token, table number, cart, special instructions, coupon code, favourite dishes and display preference (light or dark) in your phone's or browser's local storage, so they survive a reload. You can remove them by clearing the app's or website's data.
- **Camera:** used only to scan the QR code. The camera picture is processed on your device and is never uploaded or stored.
- **Network type:** the mobile app notices when your phone changes network (Wi-Fi, mobile data or none) so it can check access again. This happens on your device and is not sent to us.
- **Server logs:** like any internet service, our servers and hosting provider see your internet address and the requests your device makes. These are used to run, secure and troubleshoot the service (for example to limit abuse) and are kept only as long as needed for that.

## 6. Hotel Wi-Fi and IP Address Validation

When the restaurant switches on its Wi-Fi restriction, the server compares the internet (IP) address of your request with the restaurant's own internet connection each time you order or start a payment. We record only whether your session passed the check and when; your IP address is not stored in our database for this purpose. The app does not read your Wi-Fi network name; it only shows the network name the restaurant has set.

## 7. Location Information

Only if the restaurant switches on its location restriction (ordering allowed only near the restaurant), the app asks for permission to use your device's location while you order. It then reads your position (latitude, longitude, the device's estimate of its accuracy, when it was measured and, on Android, whether it came from a mock-location app) when you open the app, when you return to it and when you place an order, and sends it to our server. The server only measures how far you are from the restaurant and allows or refuses the order; your position is not stored, not logged and not shared. If the restriction is off, the app never asks for or reads your location. You can refuse or withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's or browser's settings; you will then not be able to order through the app while the restriction is on, but staff can take your order.

## 8. How We Use Customer Information

We use the information above only to:

- take, prepare, serve and track your orders;
- produce your bill, take and confirm payments, and issue refunds;
- apply offers and coupons;
- check, when switched on, that dine-in ordering happens on the restaurant's Wi-Fi and from within the permitted distance of the restaurant;
- keep a record of your acceptance of our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy;
- read your feedback to improve our food and service;
- keep the accounting and tax records the law requires, and prevent fraud and misuse.

We do not use your information for advertising or marketing, we do not build profiles of you, and we do not sell it.

## 9. Payment Information and Third-Party Payment Providers

- When you pay online, the payment is handled by the restaurant's payment provider (for UPI payments made through it) or by your own UPI app (for UPI payments made directly to the restaurant's UPI ID). You enter your payment details there, under their own privacy policies.
- We receive and keep the payment result: amount, method, status, the provider's payment and order references, and any UPI transaction reference you or the cashier enter. We never receive or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank password.
- When the payment provider is used, we send it the amount, a reference for your order and an anonymous customer reference made from your session identifier, together with the restaurant's own contact details. We do not send your name, phone number or email address.

## 10. Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored in the restaurant's database at its hosting provider and sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access by staff is protected by individual accounts and limited by role, and important staff actions are logged. Session tokens are signed and expire after a few hours. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

## 11. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We share information only:

- with the payment provider, as described in section 9, when you pay through it;
- with service providers who host and run the service for us (servers and database), who may process it only on our instructions;
- when the law requires it, for example with tax authorities, or to protect the rights and safety of guests and staff.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

## 12. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The website version stores the items listed in section 5 in your browser's local storage only so that the app works; this information is not used to track you.

## 13. Data Retention and Deletion

- Orders, bills, payments and refunds are kept for as long as the restaurant must keep accounting and tax records under applicable law.
- Session records and records of your acceptance of these documents are kept for as long as the orders they relate to, as evidence of the terms that applied.
- Information on your device stays until you clear the app's or website's data, or the session expires.
- Because we do not collect your name or contact details, we may need your order number or bill number to find your information if you ask us to delete or correct it. Information we must keep by law cannot be deleted before that period ends.

## 14. Customer Privacy Rights

Depending on the law that applies to you (for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the right to:

- ask what information we hold about you and how it is used;
- ask us to correct or complete inaccurate information;
- ask us to delete information we no longer need or are not required to keep;
- withdraw your consent where we rely on it (this does not affect what was done before; we may then be unable to take orders from you through the app, but staff can serve you in person);
- nominate someone to exercise your rights if you are unable to;
- complain to us, and if you are not satisfied, to the data protection authority.

To use these rights, contact us as described in section 17.

## 15. Children's Privacy

The service is meant for adults ordering at the restaurant. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Children may order only with the involvement of a parent or guardian, who is responsible for the order and its payment.

## 16. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy. Each update gets a new version number and is shown in the app. When a change significantly affects how your information is used, we ask you to read and acknowledge the new version before your next order. The current version is always available from the app.

- Address: {{restaurant.address}}

- Email: {{restaurant.email}}

We will respond as soon as possible and within the time the law requires. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live (in India, the Data Protection Board of India).
